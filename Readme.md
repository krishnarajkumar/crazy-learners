# System Design & Architecture Study Plan

## Overview
This guide outlines a structured study plan to master **System Design & Architecture**, along with other key topics, which are critical for the **Morgan Stanley Director - Production Support Engineer (Level-3)** role. The plan includes carefully selected books to balance **deep theoretical understanding** and **practical application** across multiple domains.

## Books to Study

### 1. **System Design & Architecture**
📖 **Designing Data-Intensive Applications (DDIA) – Martin Kleppmann**  
🔹 Covers **scalability, reliability, maintainability, and distributed systems** concepts.

#### ✅ Key Chapters to Focus On:
- **Chapter 1:** Reliable, Scalable, Maintainable Applications
- **Chapter 2:** Data Models and Query Languages
- **Chapter 3:** Storage and Retrieval
- **Chapter 5:** Replication (Critical for High Availability)
- **Chapter 6:** Partitioning (Handling Large-Scale Data)
- **Chapter 7:** Transactions (Ensuring Data Consistency)

📖 **System Design Interview – An Insider’s Guide (Vol 1 & 2) – Alex Xu**  
🔹 Focuses on **hands-on practical case studies** of real-world systems.

#### ✅ Key System Designs to Study:
- **URL Shortener**
- **Distributed Cache (Redis/Memcached)**
- **Design YouTube (High-scale video streaming)**
- **Messaging Queue (Kafka/RabbitMQ)**
- **Distributed File System (Google Drive/Dropbox)**

---

### 2. **Distributed Systems & Scalability**
📖 **Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) – Google SRE Team**  
🔹 Covers **operational aspects of large-scale distributed systems**, including **monitoring, incident management, and reliability**.

#### ✅ Key Topics:
- Principles of Site Reliability Engineering
- Service-Level Objectives (SLOs) and Indicators (SLIs)
- Incident Response and Postmortems
- Capacity Planning and Load Balancing

---

### 3. **Microservices & Cloud Architecture**
📖 **Building Microservices – Sam Newman**  
🔹 Covers **service communication, deployment strategies, scaling, and fault tolerance**.

#### ✅ Key Topics:
- Service Decomposition & API Design
- Event-Driven Architectures
- Scaling Microservices & Distributed Transactions
- Observability (Logging, Tracing, Monitoring)

---

### 4. **Production Readiness & Incident Management**
📖 **Release It! – Michael T. Nygard**  
🔹 Covers **failure modes, resilience strategies, and production issues**.

#### ✅ Key Topics:
- Stability Patterns (Circuit Breaker, Bulkhead, Fail-Fast)
- Chaos Engineering & Fault Injection
- Deployments & Rollbacks
- Real-world Case Studies

---

### 5. **Low-Level System Understanding (OS, Performance)**
📖 **Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces – Remzi H. Arpaci-Dusseau**  
🔹 Covers **Concurrency, Virtual Memory, and Scheduling**, which impact **performance and troubleshooting**.

#### ✅ Key Topics:
- Process Management & Scheduling
- Concurrency & Synchronization
- Memory Management & Paging
- File Systems & Storage Optimization

---

## Study Plan (2 Months)

📅 **Week 1-2:** Read **DDIA** – Focus on **Replication, Partitioning, Transactions, and Storage Models**  
📅 **Week 3:** Read **System Design Interview Vol 1** – Practice **fundamental designs**  
📅 **Week 4:** Read **SRE Book** – Focus on **incident response, monitoring, and reliability**  
📅 **Week 5:** Read **Building Microservices** – Focus on **service design, scaling, and deployment**  
📅 **Week 6:** Read **Release It!** – Focus on **failure patterns and production readiness**  
📅 **Week 7:** Read **Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces** – Focus on **performance, concurrency, and scheduling**  
📅 **Week 8:** **Mock System Design Interviews, Hands-on practice, and Revision**  

---

## Expected Outcomes
✔ Gain **deep knowledge of system design and distributed systems**.  
✔ Be **interview-ready** with hands-on **system design problem-solving skills**.  
✔ Understand **scalability, fault tolerance, and real-world architecture trade-offs**.  
✔ Be **better prepared for the Morgan Stanley role** with a strong grasp of **production support challenges**.
